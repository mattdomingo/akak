Acacia is a fraternity committed to developing men of principle, service, leadership, and lifelong friendship.

Our story

A chapter with deep roots

Acacia’s Wisconsin chapter is part of a tradition that began with a simple conviction: a fraternity can pair close brotherhood with character, scholarship, and useful service. At UW–Madison, generations of brothers have carried that idea forward—forming friendships on campus, serving their community, and staying connected long after graduation.

Chapter & fraternity history

The Acacia story

1904

Acacia is founded

Acacia Fraternity is established on May 12, 1904, at the University of Michigan. Its founders envisioned a national fraternity grounded in friendship, high standards, and the fraternal ideals shared among its early members.

Early 20th century

Acacia reaches Wisconsin

The Wisconsin chapter joins Acacia’s growing national brotherhood, bringing its principles to the University of Wisconsin–Madison. The chapter becomes a home for students seeking both campus community and a lasting alumni network.

Through the decades

Generations leave their mark

Like the university and the city around it, the chapter has changed with each generation. Brothers have balanced academics, campus involvement, service, and the traditions that turn a college house into a brotherhood.

Today

Continuing the work

Acacia Wisconsin continues to welcome students who want to lead with integrity, invest in their community, and build friendships that outlast their time at Madison.
